Methods

  • Chart series come from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, retrieved November 24, 2026. Markets data run through Nov 20 (Nasdaq Nov 19).
  • Year over year is the 12-month percent change in a price index; month over month is the 1-month change.
  • October 2025 household-survey data were not collected during the federal government shutdown, so that month is blank.
  • Markets (06): 10-year breakeven = nominal − real (TIPS) yield; stocks rebased to Oct 28 = 100; ICE BofA option-adjusted spreads in basis points.
  • Taylor rule (09): i = r* + π + 0.5(π − 2) + w × 2(u* − u), with w = 0.5 (Taylor 1993) or 1.0 (balanced approach); the factor 2 converts the unemployment gap to an output gap (Okun's law). Defaults use the FOMC's September longer-run medians: fed funds 3.2% (so r* = 1.2) and unemployment 4.2%. Gradual: ρ × today's midpoint + (1 − ρ) × rule.
  • Projections (10): FOMC September medians; fed funds at year-end. December's step on the rate charts is our proposal (or the pause alternative), placed at the December meeting.
